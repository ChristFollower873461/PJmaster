# Family Start Here

If you are reading this during a stressful moment, slow down. This repo exists so you
do not have to solve everything at once.

## First 24 Hours

1. Make sure everyone is physically safe.
2. Call the person listed as the primary family coordinator in the private vault.
3. Find the private vault manifest. It should tell you where the real private records
   are stored.
4. Do not cancel phone, email, cloud, or password-manager accounts yet. Those accounts
   may be needed to recover information.
5. Do not sell, wipe, reset, or discard computers, phones, drives, notebooks, or paper
   files until someone has checked them.
6. Start a written decision log. Use [docs/FAMILY_DECISION_LOG.template.md](docs/FAMILY_DECISION_LOG.template.md).

## First Week

1. Identify the legal decision-maker or executor.
2. Contact the attorney, accountant, insurance agent, and financial advisor listed in
   the private vault.
3. Gather IDs, estate documents, insurance policies, business documents, keys, devices,
   and active bills.
4. Photograph physical papers before moving them.
5. Keep receipts for travel, funeral, repair, storage, and professional expenses.
6. Ask for help before touching anything that looks like business infrastructure,
   customer data, taxes, legal records, or crypto.

## What This Repo Can Tell You

- The shape of the family handoff plan.
- What kinds of documents exist and where they should be indexed.
- Which checklists to follow first.
- How to update or rebuild the printed CD package.

## What This Repo Cannot Tell You

This public repo should not contain the actual private details. Look for those in the
private vault, safe, attorney records, password manager, or other locations named in the
private vault manifest.

## If The CD Is All You Have

If you only have a printed CD:

1. Open the disc on any computer.
2. Open `README.md`.
3. Open this file.
4. Look for `PRIVATE_VAULT_MANIFEST.template.md`; if it has been filled privately on a
   separate copy, use that private copy.
5. If the CD only contains templates, use it as a map and ask the closest family
   coordinator where the private binder or vault is stored.
